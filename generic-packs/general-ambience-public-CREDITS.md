## Processing

Mono 48 kHz OGG Vorbis q4. Ordinary legacy one-shots were silence-trimmed and normalized with short-recovery compression and a look-ahead limiter to approximately -16 LUFS. Later recordings target -20 LUFS and synthesized musical cues approximately -18 LUFS. Per-clip exceptions, processing and attribution follow. No human listening QA is claimed. Edits do not change the upstream license.
